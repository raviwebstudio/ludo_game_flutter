GDPR & Play Store Compliant
Effective Date: June 12, 2026  ·  App: Ludo Elite  ·  Developer: Crowcent  ·  Package: com.clikmagnet.ludo_game

Ludo Elite is a mobile board game application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using Ludo Elite, you agree to this policy.

1 Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Display name and email address (when creating an account)
  • Profile avatar (optional, chosen from device gallery)
  • Google account information (if you sign in with Google)

Information collected automatically:

  • Game statistics — total games played, wins, win streak
  • Coins balance and XP level
  • Device type and operating system version (for crash reporting)
  • Firebase Analytics events (anonymous usage data)

Information we do NOT collect:

  • Location data
  • Contacts or phone book
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Payment or financial information

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your player account
  • To save and sync your game progress and statistics across devices
  • To display your username and avatar in gameplay
  • To authenticate you securely via Firebase Auth
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs using crash reports
  • To provide customer support when you contact us

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third parties.

3 Firebase & Google Services

Ludo Elite uses the following Google / Firebase services:

  • Firebase Authentication — secure login via email or Google account
  • Cloud Firestore — stores your profile data and game statistics
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage analytics to improve the app

These services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4 Data Storage & Security

  • Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase servers
  • Local game data is stored on your device via SharedPreferences
  • We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Access to your data is restricted to authorised personnel only
  • We retain your data as long as your account is active

5 Children's Privacy

Ludo Elite is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

7 Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by:

  • Going to Profile → Settings → Delete Account within the app
  • Emailing us at privacy@crowcent.com with subject "Delete My Account"

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All game data, statistics, and profile information will be permanently removed within 30 days.

8 Third-Party Links

Ludo Elite may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of Ludo Elite after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Questions or Concerns?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please reach out to us.
